cocoex · compounds co-exist

cocoex is a non-profit lab where artists, creators and collectors turn art into real environmental and social change.

The creation belongs to the artists. The impact belongs to the world.

Lunes

The Moon has pulled the tides since before memory. Lunes guards the bond between humanity and the one element no life survives without.

Ares

Mercury, ancient god of fire and conflict. Ares turns destruction into regeneration — wounded land into living forest.

Rabu

Mercury, the messenger who crossed every boundary. Rabu speaks for those whose voices don't carry — across every border, every system.

Thunor

Jupiter's thunder long preceded our ability to harness it. Thunor carries that primal force into a future where clean energy belongs to all.

Shukra

Venus sees beauty where others see utility. Shukra protects the irreplaceable complexity of life — every species a thread in a web we barely understand.

Dosei

Saturn moves with the patience of seasons and harvests. Dosei holds the quiet conviction that feeding the world is not impossible — only unfinished.

Solis

The Sun, source of all warmth and life. Solis holds the belief that human flourishing is not a privilege — it is a right.

Muse — the seven causes

Seven causes.
One constellation.

Each Muse its own community, its own body of art — the framework behind every cocoex campaign.

Comet Collab is an ecosystem where artists, collectors, activists, and communities converge through Stardust and Horizon.
Guided by Muse, in a continuous loop of creation and impact.
