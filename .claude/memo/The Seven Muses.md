# The Seven Muses
*Seven classical planets · one constellation*

Muse is not a programme. It is not a campaign. It is the thematic and curatorial orientation that runs through everything — seven causes, seven colours, seven growing bodies of art. Each Muse is becoming its own community, eventually its own DAO.

Every campaign is born under a Muse. That Muse's colour runs through all campaign branding. The Muse colour is also a brief parameter for artists — how much it lives in the artwork is the artist's decision.

## Notes

- Muse colour travels with its Muse across all campaigns and assets
- Gradients using Muse colours are decorative and atmospheric — never structural
- Individual Muse symbols are hand-drawn by MEMO — to be provided per campaign
- Muse DAOs are aspirational / in development, not a current operational fact
- Open question: are all seven Muses intended for simultaneous activation, or do they serve as a legitimising framework for focused work?
