# Horizon

Community-led campaigns within [[Comet Collab]]. Begins with a **Future Lab** — a participatory workshop inspired by Robert Jungk's Zukunftswerkstatt methodology — where the community shapes the direction before any artistic production begins. The art then interprets and carries the community's visions.

Horizon contains one step [[Stardust]] does not: the Future Lab. Shared steps use identical logic to Stardust.

---

## The Future Lab

Participatory workshop methodology. The community defines the vision. Then art is made from it.

Reference: Robert Jungk's *Zukunftswerkstatt* (Future Workshop).
