# Stardust

Creator-led campaigns within [[Comet Collab]]. An artist is activated through an open call or direct invitation. The campaign moves outward from the artist's work. cocoex builds the conditions; the artist makes the work and leaves the trail.

## How it works

1. Artist is selected via open call or direct invitation
2. cocoex builds the campaign conditions and infrastructure
3. Artist makes the work autonomously — creative autonomy is absolute
4. Comets (physical/digital assets) are produced and sold
5. Proceeds fund the partner NGO via transparent on-chain splits
6. The artist's passage leaves Stardust — proof something meaningful moved through
